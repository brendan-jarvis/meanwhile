# meanwhile — header / logo brief

For an image generator (ChatGPT Images etc.) or a designer. Target:
GitHub social preview 1280×640, also usable as a README header.

## One-line

A wordmark that looks like it is decoding out of horizontal matrix rain —
caught at the exact moment the code becomes readable.

## Prompt (paste-ready)

> A wide dark banner (1280×640) for a terminal app called "meanwhile".
> Near-black background (#070907). Horizontal streams of small half-width
> katakana and digits flow left to right across the frame in shades of
> phosphor green (#22c55e down to #052e16), like Matrix code rain rotated
> 90 degrees — with soft CRT glow on the brightest glyphs. In the center,
> the lowercase word "meanwhile" in a clean geometric monospace typeface,
> large, in pale luminous green-white: its first letters fully formed, its
> last two letters still half-scrambled, dissolving into katakana glyphs —
> the word is being decoded out of the stream. One thin stream passes
> through the wordmark. Flat, crisp, pixel-sharp; no 3D, no bevels, no
> lens flare, no people, no pills, no film imagery. The mood: calm,
> ambient, slightly reverent — a machine quietly telling the truth.

## Rules

- Lowercase "meanwhile", always. Monospace or near-monospace type.
- Green on near-black is the canonical look; a variant may adopt any
  terminal theme palette (the app itself does this on Omarchy).
- The decode moment is the identity: some of the word readable, some
  still code. Never fully clean, never fully scrambled.
- No Matrix trademark imagery (no pills, characters, or film stills).
- Small-size check: the word must survive at 320px wide.
